Our Approach

How we build apps

The same four rules apply to everything we publish.

On-device by default

Recordings, location and finances are processed and stored on your phone. None of our apps has an account system or a server that holds your data.

Works offline

Every core feature works with no connection at all. The network is used only to show ads.

Honest about ads

Our apps are free and supported by Google AdMob ads. Your recordings and transactions are never part of an ad request.

Permissions explained

Every permission an app asks for is listed and explained in its privacy policy, in plain language.
