How it works

How to record video with the screen off

Most camera apps stop the moment the display turns off, because the recording is tied to what's on screen. Screen Off Recorder runs the camera from a background service instead, so the display can go dark and the recording keeps going.

  1. Choose your settings Front or rear camera, SD, HD or Full HD, audio on or off, and an optional date, time or location stamp. Set a default, or leave it on "Always ask" to choose before each recording.
  2. Tap Record Recording starts and a "Recording in progress" notification appears. It stays visible for as long as the camera is running.
  3. Turn the screen off Lock the phone, put it in a pocket or on a stand, or switch to another app. The recording carries on in the background.
  4. Pause or stop whenever you like Use the buttons in the app or in the notification. Pausing and resuming continues the same video rather than starting a new file.
  5. Find it in Clips When you stop, the segments are merged into one MP4 and you'll see progress while it saves. The finished video shows up in Clips automatically.
