Getting started

How to track expenses and stick to a budget

Most budgeting apps fail on the second week, when logging every coffee starts to feel like work. This one is built to keep the typing down, so the numbers are still accurate at the end of the month.

  1. Open it and start There's no account and nothing to sign up for. A starter Cash account and a set of common categories are ready on first launch.
  2. Add your accounts Cash, bank, cards, wallets and savings, each with its own live balance. Moving money between them is a transfer, so it isn't counted as spending or income.
  3. Set a budget Enter a monthly budget, plus an expected income if you want one. The summary card shows where you stand and warns you before you overspend.
  4. Log as you go Type it in on the keypad, scan a receipt, or turn on notification import and confirm the drafts. Rent, EMIs and subscriptions only need setting up once, as recurring transactions.
  5. Review the month Filter by day, week, month, year or a custom range. The category pie chart and top-spend breakdown show where the money went.

Everything you'd expect from a budget app, without handing your financial data to anyone.

Expense & income tabs

Separate tabs, each with a live summary card. Amounts are colour-coded red for expense, green for income and blue for transfer.

Cash, bank, cards, wallets and savings, with live balances and transfers between them.

Rent, bills, EMIs and subscriptions, with an optional reminder before each one falls due.

Notification import

Optional. Payment notifications from bank and UPI apps become drafts on your phone, and you confirm each one.

Encrypted and locked

Google Drive backup

An encrypted backup to your own Google Drive, daily at a time you choose or with one tap. New phone? Connect the same account and restore everything.
