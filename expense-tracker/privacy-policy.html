Expense Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Overview

Expense Tracker (the “App”, package com.nitcart.expensetracker) is a personal finance app designed to help you manage transactions, budgets, accounts, and categories on your device.

Short version: Your financial information — transactions, accounts, budgets — stays on your phone and is never sent to the developer. If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy is saved to your own Google Drive, which the developer cannot access. The App is free and supported by ads (Google AdMob), which use a device advertising identifier.

Permissions & Data Access

CAMERA

Used only when you choose to scan a receipt. Text recognition runs on-device and the photo is not uploaded.

NOTIFICATIONS

Used to show recurring-bill reminders and import notifications. Optionally, you can grant notification access so the App reads your bank and UPI app transaction alerts to create draft expenses for your review — this is off by default.

Processed only on your device. Any notification content used for auto-import is read and parsed on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, sold, or shared, and you can turn this off at any time.

BIOMETRIC

Used only if you turn on app lock to protect access to the App.

INTERNET

Used to serve ads (see “Advertising” below) and, only if you turn it on, to back up to and restore from your Google Drive (see “Backup & restore”). Everything else — tracking, budgets, receipt scanning and any import — works fully offline, and your transactions, accounts and budgets are never sent to the developer.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Transactions and account data — Your expenses, income, categories, accounts, budgets, and recurring items are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device.
  • App preferences — Settings such as currency, theme, app lock, and import preferences are stored locally and are not shared with the developer.
  • Exports — If you choose to export your data as a CSV file, that file is created only when you request it and is shared only where you choose to send it.

Data Transmission

The App does not have its own account system or backend. It does not collect, sell, or share your personal or financial information — your transactions, accounts, categories and budgets — with the developer or any third party.

Receipt scanning and any notification-based import are handled on your device, and none of that content is uploaded as part of normal App use. Data leaves your device in only two cases: what the ad component (Google AdMob) needs to serve ads — see “Advertising” below — and, if you turn on backup, an encrypted backup sent to your own Google Drive (see “Backup & restore”).

Backup & restore OPTIONAL

You can back up your data to your own Google Drive — manually with “Back up now”, or automatically once a day at a time you choose — and restore it on this or a new phone by connecting the same Google account. Backup is off until you connect a Google account.

  • Where it goes — a private app-data folder in your Google Drive that only this App can read or write. The App asks Google for access to that folder alone (the drive.appdata permission); it cannot see any other file in your Drive. The backup is sent directly from your phone to Google and never passes through, or is visible to, the developer. Google’s handling of Drive data is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • What it contains — your transactions, accounts, categories, recurring rules and app settings. Receipt images are not included. Only the most recent backup is kept; each new backup replaces the previous one.
  • Encryption — the backup is encrypted on your phone before it is uploaded, and sent over an encrypted connection. It is protected by a key built into the App rather than a password you choose, so it can be restored on any phone signed in to the same Google account.
  • Deleting it — in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find Expense Tracker and choose “Delete hidden app data”. Disconnecting the account in the App stops future backups but leaves the existing one in your Drive. You can also remove the App’s Drive access at any time from your Google Account’s security settings.
Restoring replaces everything. Restoring a backup deletes the data currently on your device and replaces it with the backup’s contents. This cannot be undone.

Advertising

The App is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may access and share a device advertising identifier and limited device/technical information (such as general location derived from IP, and ad-interaction data) with Google, in line with Google’s Privacy Policy.

The App itself does not use analytics services and does not send your financial data to advertisers. You can reset or limit ad personalisation from your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data remains on your device. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, the local information stored by the App is removed from the device. A Google Drive backup, if you made one, stays in your Drive until you delete it (see “Backup & restore”).

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to appsupport@nitcart.com.
